# Community Guidelines

> Last updated: [YYYY-MM-DD]
> Applies to: [Discord / forums / Steam discussions / subreddit — list every space you moderate]
> Owner: [community-manager]

**What this is.** The published rules of your community spaces, and the private
enforcement ladder that backs them. Players read § *The rules* and § *What happens
if you break them*; the team reads all of it, because consistent moderation is
impossible when the ladder lives in one person's head.

**Written by** the community-manager agent — it drafts and revises this, and cites
it in every moderation action.
**Read by** anyone moderating, `/team-marketing` when planning a community push,
and the producer when a moderation call needs escalating.

**How to fill it:** dispatch the community-manager agent, or write it yourself
before you open your first public space — not after the first incident. Publish
§ *The rules* verbatim wherever players can see it.

---

## The rules

*Short, plain, enforceable. Each rule is one line a player can remember, with one
line of "what this means in practice" under it. Vague rules ("don't be toxic") are
unenforceable; the practice line is what makes them real.*

| # | Rule | What this means in practice |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | [Be civil — argue about the game, not the person.] | [Criticism of a design decision is welcome. Insults aimed at a player or a dev are not.] |
| 2 | [No spoilers outside the spoiler channels.] | [Tag anything past the point marked in the pinned post.] |
| 3 | [No harassment, hate speech, or targeted pile-ons.] | [Including in DMs to other members that start here.] |
| 4 | [No advertising or self-promo without a mod's okay.] | [Sharing your own fan art or stream is fine in the channel for it.] |
| 5 | [No leaks, cheats, or piracy links.] | [Datamined content is a leak. So is an unreleased build.] |
